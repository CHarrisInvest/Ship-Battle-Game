# Promotional images

Art for ads, store listings and social posts. Nothing here is served by the site; the live share card
and icons are `public/`, drawn by `npm run icon`.

## Drawn art (`npm run promo`)

Generated by `tools/promo.mjs` with the menu's own `drawGalleon`, so redraw rather than edit by hand.
The hero is the game's mark from `tools/mark.mjs`, the Cutter light the home-screen icon is drawn
from, so an ad and the icon a player then taps are the same ship. Her guns are drawn at 1.5 times their true
size (`GUN_SCALE` in `tools/promo.mjs`), so they read at an ad's scale.
The copy names no count of ships, since the fleet will grow.

## The trailer (`npm run trailer`)

`trailer-1080x1920.mp4` is a 30-second trailer, upright, for a store listing, Shorts, Reels or a
feed. `tools/trailer.mjs` cuts it from real frames: the game runs in a headless Chromium on a clock
the tool advances a sixtieth of a second a frame, so every frame is one step of a round and the
picture is smooth however slowly the machine draws. A fight between 3rd rates with no HUD, the
title, the menu with the mark turning on her plate, a free-for-all between fully found 1st rates
with the whole HUD, one in a fully found Baltimore Clipper, and the title again with the address.
Her ship is sailed by the engine's own AI through a helm the frame loop consults, and each round
is sailed first in Node to pick the window with the most guns going off in view of her. Silent,
with an empty audio track so every uploader takes it. Re-cut it when the ships or the HUD change;
`--dry` only chooses the shots, `--quick` cuts a sketch a few seconds long, and `--keep` leaves the
frames behind. Needs ffmpeg.

3-D model, so these frames show ships that do not yet match the cutter on the art above. Retake
them once the ships at sea are drawn closer to the menu's design.

Real frames from a round of Free-for-all on a phone at 3x, sailing a fully found 1st rate. A round
is not repeatable, so these were chosen by hand from a burst. `npm run promo -- --play` now sails the
mark, and a cutter's fight reads small: she meets small ships and fires 5 guns a side. These frames
were taken under the 1st rate before the switch and are kept because they show more of the fight.
